# Impeachment appeal procedures

Status: reviewed proposal, not adopted policy. Consolidated revision 0.5 and the 0.6 filing-clock/finality supplement are the current research versions. Older 0.3 and 0.4 drafts are superseded. Meaningful usable access, outages, restrictions and actual authority bindings remain explicit parts of evaluation; a technical review does not implement or adopt these procedures.

## Part A — Appeal procedure

## 1. Authority and limits

JON-61  controls Supreme Board review, Option C independent legal review/factual deference, removal pending appeal, procedural remand versus insufficiency reversal, self-representation, layered disclosure and discrepancy review. Bootstrap specification revision 1.0 §§9–12 preserves frozen panels and the late-discovered-conflict limitation. No changes to jurisdiction, quorum, voting thresholds or substantive offenses are proposed here.
JON-67 consolidated revision 0.5-draft is a provisional interface reference, not accepted authority. Its native dependency remains. JON-79 owns franchise projection; JON-62/60 own capital/finality and terminal consequences.
Previously inspected [FRAP 3](<https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frap/rule_3>), [FRAP 10](<https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frap/rule_10>) and [FRAP 41](<https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frap/rule_41>) suggest notice, record and mandate patterns. Concord does not adopt U.S. fees, counsel, jurisdictional doctrine or federal day counts. All new procedures/numbers below remain proposals.

## 2. Proposed filing and preparation

Use a simple notice followed by a reasoned submission. Notice identifies caseId, appellant identity, judgmentId/version, intended appellate scope and Supreme Board destination; detailed legal argument is not required for the initial receipt. Express scope limitation is recorded; an accidental omission does not automatically determine waiver. Any scope/timeliness dispute goes to the competent Board under governing law.

Candidate calibration for this direct impeachment path:

* Notice window: four complete turns after actual judgment notice and access to the operative reasons and record index sufficient to identify the challenged disposition.
* Opening submission: four complete turns after notice is filed and the meaningful party-access record is available.
* Response: four complete turns after actual access to the opening submission and cited record.
* Reply: two complete turns after response access, confined to responsive matters.
  These are proposed preparation intervals, not recovered deadlines or mandatory adjudication deadlines. They intentionally separate simple filing from evidence-dependent preparation. Do not copy JON-62's ordinary/C4 three/two-turn windows into this path; if a more specific controlling rule applies, preserve it and record the scope distinction before adoption.

Count complete turns from the first authoritative boundary after both triggering conditions are satisfied; store both receipts. The receipt service must accept a timely submission up to the defined closing boundary under a published phase order. Failed or disputed access cannot be silently treated as successful service. A documented access outage pauses the affected preparation clock; restoration records the remaining complete turns. A source contradiction goes to discrepancy review.
A competent Board may give a reasoned extension for materially impaired self-representation/access or an authorized exceptional circumstance; no assumed automatic extension. These mechanics need calibration review and must not reduce a controlling protected floor.
Receive attempted filings even if late or malformed, recording the original time and defect. Permit form correction; never backdate receipt. The authorized decision-maker determines timeliness and any relief; the clerk does not invent jurisdiction or destroy an attempted appeal. Removal remains effective throughout.

### Filing-service outage and attempted-submission evidence — proposed procedure (F68-1)

A failure of the authorized filing/receipt service is distinct from missing record access. For each affected submission, retain the intended destination, judgment/version, attempted submission content digest and protected original, actual local attempt time/phase and its clock/source, available transport/error evidence, any service incident reference, actual successful receipt time if one later exists, and both parties' opportunity to contest the timing claim. A local attempt log is evidence of a claim, not proof of server receipt or timeliness; missing server receipt is not proof that no attempt occurred.

For independently established service unavailability that prevents the party from filing through an available authorized route, the candidate rule pauses that affected filing window (including the notice window) from the established outage point. Preserve the allowance already consumed and the remaining usable complete turns. An interrupted unusable turn is not charged as a completed available turn. Resume the remaining allowance after verified restoration and notice of restored access under the published simulation boundary/phase order; do not restart the entire window or shorten the unused allowance. Availability of an unannounced, unauthorized or unusable alternate route is not presumed. No new provider or filing channel is authorized by this draft.

A disputed outage or unverifiable attempt is recorded as a pending timeliness question, not automatically granted a pause or rejected as late. The competent Board determines whether the pause applies and any lawful relief, citing source evidence and governing rules. An unresolved filing/outage claim material to timeliness prevents an affirmative finality assertion for the affected judgment until actual authorized disposition; a clerk/checker cannot dispose of it merely because the usual closing boundary passed. Record uncertain status explicitly; an allegation alone does not establish a completed or timely filing, or a stay of removal.

If the service supplies no receipt before the closing boundary, receive the preserved attempt/evidence when submission is possible and record that actual receipt separately. Never backdate server receipt. A later legal ruling that the attempted filing satisfies timeliness is a separately attributable decision, not alteration of the receipt. No original submission is silently replaced by a later merits revision.

Clock implementation must publish phase ordering and source-of-time verification before use, including same-boundary failure/restoration. This candidate outage rule is reviewable procedure, not recovered law or a claim that a reliable receipt service exists. Removal remains effective pending appeal; this process creates no automatic restoration or new terminal authority.

## 3. Immutable record and correction

Freeze a record manifest: case/judgment/version, charges/counts, governing ruleset, panel/snapshot references, notices/access receipts, procedural decisions, ballots/findings, admitted evidence, party submissions, disclosure decisions and judgment.
Maintain separate public procedural index, protected party-access package and authorized judicial record. A hash establishes integrity, not admissibility, completeness or access authorization.
Each item has sourceId, source version/digest, admission status, visibility authority and inclusion reason. Preserve both parties' correction requests and objections. A correction is a new manifest version; originals remain intact. Record whether an item was actually before the trial body. Reconstruct missing material from attributable retained sources, with a contestable difference report.
Newly offered proof is not silently inserted into the trial record. A missing existing exhibit and new evidence to repair failed proof are different. Record reconstruction cannot cure legally insufficient prosecution evidence by invention.
If protected redaction prevents meaningful response, provide enough lawful substance or do not rely on that material against the accused. Public disclosure does not follow merely from defense access. No separate lawyer/representative is introduced.

## 4. Review and remedies

The existing Supreme Board's lawful composition, recusal and voting rules apply; lack of a lawful body is not permission to lower thresholds or delegate merits to a checker.
For each ground, record: challenged count/order, governing rule/version, cited record, classification (legal, procedural, factual sufficiency), adopted review standard, reasoned finding and disposition. Legal/constitutional questions receive independent review. Factual findings receive deference unless clearly unsupported under the governing record and evidentiary standard. Different preferred weighting alone is insufficient. No automatic full retrial.
Late-discovered investigative participation alone does not cause automatic removal/reversal under the frozen bootstrap rule; separately applicable misconduct or legal violations require their own lawful analysis.

| Disposition | Required explanation/output |
| -- | -- |
| Affirm | Grounds resolved and precise surviving judgment/counts. |
| Procedural reversal/remand | Identified defect, affected counts, permitted remand work and preserved matters. Retrial is permitted only as the lawful disposition provides. |
| Insufficiency reversal | Affected conviction ends; no retrial merely to fill the evidentiary gap. |
| Mixed/partial disposition | Separate each count; explain separability and dependencies without assuming unaffected punishment or disqualification. |
| Record/authority discrepancy | Preserve sources and restrict only implicated disputed authority pending actual competent independent review. |

Do not invent a harmless-error test, new prosecution appeal, evidentiary burden or collateral review right to complete this table. A case needing an absent controlling rule records the precise gap; other grounds can still be drafted/assessed.

## 5. Judgment, finality and interfaces

Separate: received notice, complete record, submissions, Board judgment, any lawfully available correction/rehearing, and authenticated final disposition. The draft does not create a new merits rehearing entitlement. If governing law supplies one, its actual window/filings/disposition must be included; otherwise no fictional pending opportunity is asserted.
Proposed AppealDisposition fields: eventId, caseId, judgmentId/version, priorJudgmentRef, rulesetVersion, competentAuthorityEvidenceRef, votes/decisionRef, affectedCounts, remedy, remandScope, sourceRecordDigest, effectiveBoundary/phase, pendingLawfulReviewOrStayRefs and payloadDigest.
A finality record cites actual notice/access, applicable opportunities, filings, rulings, outstanding stays and authority. Unknown opportunity/access/stay status prevents an affirmative finality assertion; it is not zero. The finality issuer's competence must be verified from existing law before implementation; fields alone confer no power.
Deliver the disposition to JON-67's restriction domain for per-scope reconciliation and to other authorized domain owners. Neither filing nor reversal automatically reinstates office, voting rights or superseded restrictions. Separate succession/eligibility rules and lawful surviving consequences control. The provisional JON-67 0.5 deadline model is consumed explicitly: if mandatory review was missed, LAPSED_REVIEW prevents reactivation even when original expiresAt has not yet arrived. Reversal does not retrospectively cure that lapse; any new restriction needs prospective lawful authority and current required review. Other independent lawful bases remain intact.
Capital or terminal effects require JON-62/60's separate full conditions, including applicable appeal opportunities, valid conviction/finality, absence of stays and administrative interval. This draft's appellate finality record is evidence for those gates, not a terminal authorization.

Delivery retries use the same event identity/digest. Reading a stable accepted record twice must not duplicate consequences. Duplicate legal transitions (distinct from authenticated committed-event retries under Part B), conflicting payloads, stale authority or incompatible disposition ordering preserve all evidence and trigger actual independent discrepancy review, not silent overwrite.

## Part B — Appeal result and eligibility interface

## 1. Four separate facts

1. The competent Board issues an attributable judgment/disposition.
2. That judgment becomes legally effective under its governing rule, with any distinct finality/stay evidence.
3. Each receiving authority durably commits its lawful domain consequence or records that no consequence applies.
4. Each required downstream consumer applies its committed notification.
   Neither transmission nor an eligibility receipt proves all four. A judgment can be final while a delivery obligation is pending. Conversely, a durable receipt cannot make an invalid judgment final. No automatic terminal action follows either fact.

## 2. Proposed immutable disposition envelope

Preserve sourceSystem/sourceEventId, canonicalizationVersion/digest, schemaVersion, caseId, judgmentId/version, priorJudgmentRef, decisionAuthorityRef, decision/vote evidence, rulesetVersion, trialRecordManifestDigest, subject identities with JON-78 registration references, sourceDecisionSequence, causalSourceIds and legal effective boundary/phase.
Describe each count separately: countId, originalFindingRef, disposition, reason class, survivingFindingRefs and remand limits. Identify the exact affected order/consequence references; an empty or missing list does not mean revoke everything.
Separate public procedural summary from protected judicial/party evidence. An evidence reference does not grant access. Do not export trial evidence to ballot consumers when verified effect/provenance references suffice.
Record stay/finality status and authority references separately; unknown is explicit. No caller-supplied final=true, signature or hash can replace verification of competent legal authority.

The final row identifies a non-implemented/unresolved mapping, not a proposed change to the meaning of permanent franchise loss. Ordinary impeachment conviction is not automatically a felony event. This research cannot supply an absent higher-order rule.

For every reversal/remand, check both original expiry and the current mandatory-review deadline from JON-67 0.5 Part A. An unexpired order whose review deadline was missed cannot reactivate. Preserve its lapse and any gap; a new prospectively lawful order is distinct from resurrection. An appeal event does not extend either deadline.

## 4. Retry, correction and supersession

Apply Part B of the provisionally pinned JON-67 0.5 proposed delivery distinction: an unchanged authenticated event retry returns its original durable result with no new transition; conflicting content or duplicate/stale legal transitions retain discrepancy review.
A corrected judgment is a new legally attributable version/event with an explicit relationship to the prior judgment. Never mutate the content under the same source key. A harmless publication correction must not quietly become a different merits disposition.
Define effect identity from source judgment/version, recipient domain, verified subject/scope and semantic mapping version, so the same judgment cannot be independently applied twice through JON-67 and a direct JON-68 route. JON-79 owns deduplication/admission. A new mapping version alone is not legal authority for another consequence; preserve links to previous derivations and require its lawful review before changed effect.
Each domain receipt identifies the source, result, committed version/sequence and downstream obligation. Keep receipt authorization scoped; retrying a privileged old request does not grant current access to protected results or permission to create new effects.

## 5. Ordering, concurrency and partial delivery

Legal effective sequence, observed arrival and durable commit order must remain distinct. A later judgment arriving first cannot authorize guessing the missing predecessor or discarding the older judgment as mere network noise.
JON-79's event/result and downstream obligation must commit together in its authoritative transaction. This is not a promise that office, eligibility and all ballot systems commit simultaneously. Each effect tracks its own COMMITTED/ALREADY_COMMITTED/REJECTED/PENDING_RECONCILIATION outcome and separate consumer-delivery status.
If one domain commits and another is pending, report partial application and preserve causal links. Do not roll back a lawful judgment, cancel a pending approved operation, or manufacture a new corrective sanction to make statuses match.
For ballot acceptance/close, require the JON-79/80/81 contract's authoritative version/watermark and serialization evidence. Accepted valid ballots survive later disqualification. A restoration after close does not reopen an election; before-close return to the unvoted group requires an otherwise eligible opening-roll nonvoter. Producer-side timestamps, a provisional draft or eventual notification cannot decide a disputed race.
Unknown precedence or conflicting sources produce an attributable discrepancy and actual independent resolution, limited to the implicated authority. No silent retrospective edit to accepted-ballot history.

## 6. Finality boundary

The appellate record must identify the decision-maker competent to attest finality, applicable opportunities and actual notice/access, filings/dispositions, active stays and administrative requirements. These are checked evidence, not inferred from time alone. Include any unresolved material filing-service outage/timeliness claim and its eventual authorized disposition under Part A §2; the absence of a server receipt alone cannot supply finality.
Our reviewed sources do not establish a complete issuer/verification protocol for every direct Supreme Board finality case. This remains a named implementation/adoption gap; independent record-procedure work need not wait.
JON-62/60 retain any capital-specific finality certificate, full administrative interval, valid conviction and no-stay requirements. No eligibility response or delivery acknowledgment substitutes for those checks. Ordinary appellate finality and authorization of an irreversible effect are different records.

## 1. Filing and preparation clock contract

Retain candidate notice/opening/response/reply windows of 4/4/4/2 complete turns. These are a concrete ordinary-design recommendation, not recovered constitutional deadlines or evidence of measured workload. Each clock starts at the first boundary strictly after all its existing 0.5 triggers have occurred with verified access. A more specific adopted path takes precedence; JON-62's ordinary lower-appeal/C4 windows are not copied to this direct impeachment path.

Use JON-67's versioned boundary phases: P10 receipt cutoff, P20 attributable decisions, P30 deadline effects, P40 domain projections, P50 authorized close/open operation. A submission due at boundary b is timely received through (b,P10); a clerk cannot reject it merely because processing occurs after P10. Claimed attempts without server receipt use the 0.5 outage/timeliness process, not forged receipt time.

Proposed deterministic outage counting: at each boundary retain how many complete usable turns the affected clock has consumed. A turn interrupted by established filing unavailability or required record-access failure is not charged as a complete usable turn. Resume counting full usable turns from the first boundary strictly after verified restoration and notice of restored access. Retain consumed allowance; do not restart the entire window. Multiple incidents within the same turn cannot debit/credit it twice. The authoritative incident interval and affected party/route are recorded; a merely alleged outage awaits competent determination and cannot be treated as an established pause. Material unresolved timeliness prevents affirmative affected-judgment finality.

Preparedness validation is separate from arithmetic. At actual opening, record manifest size/complexity, protected access, practical self-representation needs, ordinary service obligations and lawful extensions. A four-turn proposal cannot override a controlling defense floor or prove that a complex case fits. Parties do not lose preparation time merely because reviewer capacity is scarce; no invented maximum decision-completion time is imposed.

Examples:

* Both notice triggers satisfied during turn 0; origin boundary 1; four complete turns end at boundary 5 P10.
* At the boundary-3 count the party has used two full turns. An outage interrupts turn 3–4; that turn is uncharged. Restoration and access notice occur during turn 4–5; counting resumes at boundary 5. Two remaining usable turns end at boundary 7 P10.
* An attempted submission at boundary 5 has no receipt because of claimed outage: retain evidence and actual later receipt separately; competent timeliness ruling, no backdating or assumed finality.
* A timely response is received at P10 and becomes usable later: the reply's two-turn clock waits for actual access and the next boundary; it does not start from the response's receipt alone.

## 3. Concrete proposed finality procedure

1. **Assemble evidence.** The judiciary's authorized record function assembles a protected packet for one exact case/judgment/version: lawful decision/vote/panel references, actual notice/access, applicable lawful appeal/correction opportunities and their source rules, filings/attempts, timeliness decisions, dispositions, current stays and any unresolved material discrepancy. Do not create a rehearing opportunity absent law.
2. **Determine legal completeness.** Disputed opportunity, timeliness, stay, jurisdiction or judgment validity is resolved by the competent judicial body under its existing authority. A clerk/checker cannot decide merits, invent jurisdiction or lower quorum. For this direct appeal the Supreme Board remains the decision source; no new external finality tribunal is created.
3. **Verify the attesting role.** The proposed ordinary operating arrangement is a judiciary-authorized record officer issuing a ministerial certificate of the competent body's determination. It may be used only with an actual attributable, versioned appointment/delegation or governing rule covering this case and attestation, verified as effective and within scope. A Board-issued record still needs actual decision authority, not just a name/signature. Without this authority evidence, disposition is NEEDS_AUTHORITY_EVIDENCE; no affirmative certificate. This arrangement is a proposal under institutional self-rule, not an assertion that the role has already been designated.
4. **Issue a versioned attestation.** Bind certificate ID/version, case/judgment/version, authority/decision references, evidence manifest digest, lawful source-rule versions, clock/phase version, covered disposition/scope, effective point and verified finality facts. Record any independent barred consequence rather than asserting blanket permission. Public metadata and protected proof remain separate.
5. **Verify at each consuming domain.** Verify authorized sender, current right to read/submit, source authority at issuance, unmodified content, correct subject/scope, known causal predecessor and unchanged relevant judgment/stay state. A hash/signature alone does not establish competence. Missing required evidence is INCOMPLETE; conflict is DISPUTED and routed for actual independent review. Never turn these into FINAL by timeout.
6. **Handle later evidence.** A later stay, corrected judgment, successful challenge or material newly received timely-filing evidence creates a new attributable record and immediate scoped re-evaluation before any pending consequence. Retain original certificate and reliance history; do not mutate old bytes or retroactively invent completed actions. A stale certificate cannot authorize a new consequence merely because it was once valid.
7. **Keep effects separate.** Certificate status (UNASSESSED, INCOMPLETE, DISPUTED, VERIFIED_FINAL, SUPERSEDED) is a proposed evidence projection, not an automatic legal judgment or a grant to restore office/franchise. A verified finality attestation never authorizes terminal action by itself. JON-62/60 still require valid applicable conviction, all actual opportunities/dispositions, no active stay and the separate complete administrative interval. They own any terminal certificate/transaction and its authority.

Authority-source gap disposition: the research now supplies exact role-evidence requirements and a candidate ministerial route. The remaining implementation input is an actual institutional role/delegation reference or more specific controlling source. There is no request to invent a new owner-level constitutional decision; if an actual conflict is found, identify that precise conflict before escalation.
