# Coin-mechanism research

The coin study is one research track within Concord's broader persistent-agent society. It investigates how agents use, modify, exchange, ignore or abandon issuer-specific instruments when no intended economic purpose is assigned to them.

Status: design specifications and research methods, with identified implementation candidates and future verification requirements. These documents do not establish a running experiment, a deployed observer or scientific results. See the [project status](../../PROJECT_STATUS.md) for the published implementation state.

Agents may use ordinary cooperation, labor/barter agreements or other objects instead. Coins do not automatically become authoritative balances, ownership, settlement or governmental authority. Refusal, voluntary return, retention and nonuse remain valid; ordinary agreements keep their separate authority.

- [Observer boundaries](OBSERVER_BOUNDARIES.md) preserves the full 17-channel leakage matrix and R1–R14 requirements. It explains how observations, identities, timing, resources, failures and researchers must remain outside participant-visible behavior.
- [Measurement and study protocol](MEASUREMENT_PROTOCOL.md) defines ten metric families, separates events from claims and interpretation, and identifies the concrete environment, sample, window, comparison and analysis choices still needed for a study.

The methodology records nonuse and missing observations separately. Neither monetary adoption nor any particular economic outcome defines success. No run, new mechanics or provider action is authorized by these documentation pages.
