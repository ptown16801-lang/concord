## CONTROLLING USER DECISION — institutional self-rule / subsidiarity

The project owner does **not** want to centrally pre-decide ordinary low-level institutional procedure. Groups, branches, divisions, agreement parties, and implementation teams should make most of their own rules within their lawful jurisdiction.

Apply this rule when resolving the children below:

* push ordinary procedural choices to the institution/group that actually bears the responsibility;
* preserve global constitutional invariants, Article 0/user supremacy, identity/population limits, due-process floors, authoritative-record requirements, security-domain boundaries, and non-amendable rules;
* a group may not use local rulemaking to enlarge its constitutional jurisdiction, evade another body's rights, alter global ballot/population accounting, or weaken protected security/record guarantees;
* technical implementation choices that do not change constitutional authority should be decided by the responsible engineering agents and validated by tests rather than escalated to the project owner;
* escalate to the project owner only when a decision changes cross-institution authority, immutable/global rights, non-amendable constraints, or an intentionally reserved constitutional choice.

Accordingly, this tracker should no longer treat every unspecified low-level procedure as a user decision.
