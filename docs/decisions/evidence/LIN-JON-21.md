## Required analytical discipline

* Compare actors on the same underlying event/proposition.
* Analyze individual trajectory over time, then immediate subgroup, parent institution, comparable groups and overall population.
* Keep formal affiliation separate from observed behavioral similarity and temporal trajectory.
* Local divergence may indicate many things; do not infer motive, loyalty, guilt or conspiracy automatically.
* Population averaging must not erase local disagreement.
* Consequential claims require independent evidence/procedure.
