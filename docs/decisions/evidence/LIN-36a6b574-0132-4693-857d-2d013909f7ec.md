## 1. Research proposition

The theory is that persistent autonomous agents with persistent, auditable histories may spontaneously assign social and economic meaning to freely issued, transferable, issuer-specific units—potentially producing trust, status, exchange value, or social capital—without the system defining those meanings or values.

This is a hypothesis, not an assigned participant goal. Non-use, heterogeneous interpretations, failed exchange, deception, gifts, status signaling and negotiated economic use are all possible observations. “Became money” is not the definition of success.

## 2. Reconstructed conversation summary

The discussions connected three research threads: persistent agent societies rather than only short-lived task swarms; the emergence of social/economic meaning around personal instruments; and reliable research coordination without duplicate workers or repeated interviews. Anthropic’s multiagent research, Generative Agents, Project Sid, Concordia, AgentSociety, and AgentSociety 2 provide related precedents, not interchangeable implementations or proof of Concord’s novelty. References B01–B06 identify those comparisons.

The coin interview deliberately removed system-defined purpose, scarcity, redemption and accounting authority. Agents start with zero whole units, create them when giving to others, and may issue freely and without a numerical cap. Objects can move to third parties, be negotiated in different quantities and combined across issuers or with other resources. There is no module-defined valuation. The proposition that indiscriminate issuance may dilute informational value is a hypothesis to measure, not a rule imposed on the system.

The conversation also separated persistent histories from automatic publicity. The coin module does not publish balances, private transfers or transaction metadata. An object normally shows its claimed original issuer and current optional marks/signatures, not a mandatory chain of holders or an agent-visible unique ID. This is privacy and incomplete provenance, not universal anonymity: issuer claims may remain visible while the transfer history is undisclosed.

Signatures have no automatic social meaning. The frozen module does not let an agent independently verify another agent’s cryptographic signature. A participant can contact a claimed signer using other communication, but that signer may lie and the module supplies no transferable verification certificate. Do not silently replace this with a conventional automatic verification API.

Wallets and private memory are conveniences, not enforced ownership. Copies, fabrication, defacing, double-spending and conflicting claims remain possible. A hidden research layer is intended to distinguish actual events from participant claims without revealing itself or intervening. That is a requirement, not evidence that perfect observation has been implemented. Actual possession, transfer, destruction and appearance boundaries still need precise definitions. Stated reasons, contemporaneous inferred intent and retrospective inferred intent must remain distinct and uncertainty-bearing.

The prior-art work already traces personal currencies, IOUs, reciprocity, token experiments, scrip, peer-to-peer incentive systems and LLM economies. Many individual ingredients have precedents. An unverified combination is not a novelty finding. The research task is to compare exact mechanisms and identify questions that remain unanswered, not to produce a novelty score from missing search hits.

## 4. Research questions and proposed analysis

**Q1 — Meaning and non-use:** What meanings do participants express or act upon? Preserve explicit statements separately from inferred labels. Silent receipt of unsolicited coins is not acceptance; no response is not necessarily refusal or indifference.

**Q2 — Issuer and object differentiation:** Do participants value claimed issuers differently, or distinguish two differently marked objects from the same issuer? Record offers, counteroffers, refusals and completed exchanges in context. Mixed compensation bundles do not automatically reveal a marginal coin price.

**Q3 — Informational dilution:** Is increased issuance or copying associated with changes in valuation, acceptance or use? Distinguish actual supply from what an agent can observe. Demand, popularity and strategic responses confound endogenous issuance; temporal sequence alone is not a causal effect.

**Q4 — Belief and authenticity:** How do claims, optional marks, private verification conversations and false reports affect behavior? Researcher detection does not establish agent awareness. A false claim can influence behavior despite being false.

**Q5 — Social structure over time:** Does circulation remain dyadic, reach third parties, concentrate, form persistent communities or disappear? Keep legitimate coin identities, their appearances, fabricated appearances, actors, dyads and runs separate. Messages within one society are not independent experimental replications.

**Q6 — Closest prior mechanisms:** Which observations are already predicted by personal currency, IOU, scrip, reciprocity, commons or explicit reputation systems? Which interactions remain untested when their redemption, custody, scarcity or semantic assumptions are removed?

These are analysis questions, not participant instructions or adopted new mechanics. First specify an observational protocol for the frozen mechanism using the existing <issue id="1aaf55ed-096e-42d1-944b-1c31f0b630ea" href="https://linear.app/jons-garage/issue/JON-105/coin-model-i-independent-experiment-and-measurement-review">JON-105</issue> work. Visibility, neutral mechanics presentation and optional signing are candidate condition families within the parent’s research boundaries; this document does not select sample sizes, conditions, statistical models or stopping rules.

A no-instrument comparator, scarcity manipulation, alternative naming condition or enforcement regime would be a separately specified comparison, not an unnoticed baseline modification. None is imposed as a new prerequisite. The selected name and even the term “coin” may evoke pretrained associations; mechanics-only instructions do not prove complete semantic neutrality. Treat this as a validity question rather than silently renaming the instrument.

## 5. Distinguish research workers from experimental participants

**Research workforce:** coordinator, source investigators, specification/instrumentation designers and independent reviewers. They may read this research packet and hidden-observer requirements. Their work should have strict artifact ownership and review.

**Experimental participants:** persistent agents whose behavior is studied. They receive only permitted participant-facing capabilities and information, never this hypothesis, researcher interpretations, hidden IDs, observer design or analyst-only labels.

A well-controlled research workflow is not permission to constrain the experimental society’s behavior. AgentSociety 2 is a useful dual-role comparison, but <issue id="99afb901-e059-4e4a-8c25-0fb01042e673" href="https://linear.app/jons-garage/issue/JON-96/emergent-issuer-specific-coin-instrument-research-model">JON-96</issue>’s agent-invisible observer requirement controls this project.
