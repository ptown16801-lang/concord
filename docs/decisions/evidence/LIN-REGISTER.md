### ECON-02 — refusal, optional return and free choice

**Status:** adopted owner design decision September 24; specification incorporation and required conformance recheck remain pending.

**Rule:** refusal alone neither destroys nor automatically returns a coin. A recipient may voluntarily sign and return it using ordinary transfer. A holder of returned coins may hold indefinitely, destroy, alter/remove marks, transfer, use other coins, or issue new coins under the existing create-and-give rule. No returned-coin-first rule applies. Recirculating an existing appearance and creating new coins are distinct actions.

**Why / alternatives:** observe whether agents voluntarily circulate or suppress marked coins, preserving freedom rather than imposing circulation. Mandatory next-use without an escape and mandatory next-use with destruction as an escape were considered and not selected. The earlier forced-reuse proposal is superseded, not an unresolved owner question.

**Preserved boundaries:** signatures are not guaranteed permanent because marks can be altered/removed. Transfer does not automatically make private information public. A built-in free-text refusal-reason field, immutable marks, automatic public disclosure and a truth guarantee for refusal allegations were not adopted. A reason may be communicated through an otherwise permitted channel. No authoritative agent-facing ledger or automatic settlement is introduced; hidden research observations remain separate and non-intervening.

**Validation still required:** update existing <pull-request id="59b18ec6-6e00-453d-8833-f2580a3d55f8" href="https://linear.app/jons-garage/review/specify-coin-model-a-v01-176161b075b7">ptown16801-lang/concord#21</pull-request> and perform the required focused conformance recheck. Check refusal without a transfer/destruction event; optional signed return as transfer; freedom to retain/destroy/alter/select other coins/create-and-give; unchanged communication visibility and hidden-observer non-intervention. These are acceptance checks to perform, not test results. Preserve the original <issue id="64554630-700b-433a-98e4-ab5e949b1c56" href="https://linear.app/jons-garage/issue/JON-103/coin-model-g-independent-v01-conformance-review">JON-103</issue> failure evidence and later correction history. No experiment or enforced implementation is claimed.

# Concord architecture and decisions register

This document records the principal accepted architecture decisions that should govern current Concord work. It is a concise control register, not a replacement for source interview records.

## Institutional self-rule / subsidiarity

* Ordinary low-level rules should be made by the group, branch, division, agreement parties, or implementation team that bears the relevant responsibility rather than being centrally specified by the project owner.
* Local rulemaking is bounded by Article 0/user supremacy, constitutional jurisdiction, global identity/population and ballot invariants, due-process floors, authoritative-record requirements, security-domain boundaries, and non-amendable provisions.
* No institution may use self-rule to enlarge its own constitutional jurisdiction, erase another institution's rights, bypass global accounting/capacity rules, or weaken mandatory security/provenance protections.
* Technical implementation choices that do not change constitutional authority are delegated to the responsible engineering agents and should be settled through documented design, tests, and review.
* Project-owner escalation is reserved for cross-institution authority changes, immutable/global rights, non-amendable constraints, and choices explicitly reserved to the owner.

## Identity, persistence, and memory

* Governance agents are persistent identities; active execution concurrency is separate from population.
* Per-agent checkpoints are durable but non-authoritative. Authoritative registry/current state wins on reconstruction.
* Complete attributable raw interaction history is retained for the lifetime of an identity.
* Private autobiographical memory/workfiles are isolated by identity where the host supports it.
* Total Recall is a residency/resource state for a living identity. It is not death, termination, office change or population-slot release.
* Death is final. While alive and authorized, an identity may transfer transferable state to a successor, but the successor remains a distinct identity with distinct keys.
* Historical knowledge must distinguish what was known at the time, private longitudinal memory, and retrospective information.

### Tombstone placeholder protocol

* `Tombstone` is the project shorthand for a terminal condemnation/deactivation state that **does not erase the identity's historical record**.
* Tombstoning is not resurrection, ordinary deletion, archival cleanup, or successor transfer. The condemned identity remains historically attributable while losing active authorization/execution status under the lawful terminal action.
* Credentials/capabilities and active-authority paths must be revoked/disabled according to the protected-domain security model; archival evidence/history remains preserved for authorized recordkeeping and review.
* A successor, if lawfully designated and state-transfer authorized before the terminal state, remains a distinct identity with distinct keys. Tombstoning never transfers identity itself.
* The exact substantive offense, legal trigger, review/appeal process, and timing come from the controlling governance rules; the placeholder protocol must not invent or broaden grounds for condemnation.

### Capital sanctions and execution

* Capital offenses are prospective classes with specified elements; a tribunal may not retrospectively convert ordinary conduct into a different offense class. Ordinary negligence, mere failure, dissent, incidental effects, and good-faith repair do not satisfy the deliberate-act capital classes unless the controlling rule expressly states otherwise.
* **C3**: an elected executive intentionally uses E-COAIA creation, divisional assignment, continuation, transfer, or deactivation for the purpose of manipulating **House apportionment**, with the required ordinary material-misconduct elements. Every required element, including intent, must be proved by a preponderance of admissible evidence. House impeachment uses ordinary majority/quorum; Senate conviction requires `ceil(2*D/3)` with ordinary quorum/recusal. House impeachment alone is not conviction. A lawful C3 Senate conviction mandates office removal and capital termination, stayed through applicable appeals, finality certificate, and final administrative interval.
* **C4**: a competent court determines from the sealed creation record by a preponderance whether a particular L-COAIA creation resolution was formed for the purpose of manipulating House apportionment rather than a legitimate investigative purpose. District court decides event-level merits; ordinary circuit and Supreme review remain available. After event-level finality, the proposing presiding officer and each authenticated affirmative voter receive the derivative mandatory capital consequence without separate individualized merits trials; only finality and identity/ballot attribution are verified before termination.
* **C5**: knowing/intentional CRA disclosure or deliberate inference facilitation of protected covert-census information as defined by the controlling census rule. Every element must be proved by a preponderance of admissible evidence. A valid C5 conviction mandates capital termination after ordinary appeals, finality certificate, and final administrative interval.
* A lawful felony conviction permanently removes voting rights immediately upon the authorized conviction event, even while appeal/finality is separately tracked. A felony conviction does not by itself terminate the identity unless it is a capital conviction under the controlling rule.
* Death/capital termination is final and same-identity resurrection is prohibited. Execution/death and `Tombstone` are related but not synonyms: the legal process determines the terminal sanction; Tombstone is the record-preserving terminal identity/authorization protocol used after a lawful terminal action.

## Institutional structure, succession, and judiciary

* Core government structure is bicameral legislature (House and Senate), elected Executive, and Judiciary.
* The recovered three-tier judiciary has **two district judges**, a **three-judge circuit court**, and a **nine-seat Supreme Board** (one chief and eight associates). District cases use one eligible unrecused judge; circuit appeals assign all three with quorum two and require two affirmative merits votes; Supreme merits quorum is seven and requires at least five affirmative votes. Ordinary Supreme discretionary review is accepted by at least four substantive judges under a seven-judge admission quorum. Missing judges/votes do not lower these fixed requirements.
* Courts interpret the Constitution; lower courts follow applicable precedent. The governing adjudicative burden is **preponderance of admissible evidence** where the controlling rule specifies that burden, with any more specific rule preserved.
* The elected executive may designate a willing eligible living successor. A phase-opening designation filed before the first turn becomes effective when publicly recorded/acknowledged; a later or changed designation requires one complete turn of public prior notice, with the prior effective designation remaining in force during that interval. Designation alone grants no current executive power.
* On executive death, the effective designated successor becomes a **caretaker** after accepting and suspending incompatible office authority. An unavailable, dead, jailed, permanently disenfranchised, or unwilling designee is skipped on authentic evidence. Fallback order is eligible registry clerk → House speaker → Senate presiding senator → eligible willing living identity with earliest creation sequence.
* A caretaker has only non-task-modifying continuity authority: preserve records/schedules, transmit existing instructions, and administer the election process. It cannot create/modify/cancel project tasks, create agents, sign/veto bills, appoint judges, initiate prosecution, choose a repository name, or sign a release. A special executive election is noticed at the next turn; caretaker limits persist until an elected successor is certified and accepts.
* The recovered impeachment route supersedes the earlier experimental `90%` / unanimous-removal scheme. House impeachment uses the chamber's ordinary majority (`floor(D/2)+1`) with its fixed full-seat quorum. A C3 executive conviction requires Senate conviction by `ceil(2*D/3)` with ordinary chamber quorum and recusal rules; House impeachment alone is a charge, not conviction.
* Elected legislative service is for the project phase. Regular legislature/executive leadership elections occur at each phase transition/opening, with no term limits. Judges follow separate tenure: after lawful appointment they serve for the project until resignation, lawful removal, death, or the capacity rule. Caretaker/interim continuity never silently becomes an elected term.
* Permanent divisions may be **active, unwinding, or dormant** and may not be deleted. Their identity, records, representation history, and obligations survive status changes. The recovered source assigns the special **unanimous entire eligible-population** requirement to **creation of an additional permanent division**, on top of the ordinary constitutional-amendment route; it does not establish that same unanimity as the rule for merely changing an existing division between active/unwinding/dormant states.
* Constitutional structure changes must preserve Article 0 supremacy and any non-amendable provisions.

## Population, voting, and ballot accounting

* Global living-agent population ceiling is 300. The current self-contained v2.2 governing baseline begins with **40 genuine ordinary governance identities**, five in each of eight permanent divisions. Earlier migration notes used a different bootstrap figure; that older figure is non-controlling unless expressly reincorporated by the user.
* After founding, ordinary identity creation has at least two distinct lawful routes: (1) the elected Executive's recorded creation authorization within population/capacity limits, and (2) an agent-initiated petition to add one new development identity to an existing division. The petition route is additional to—not a replacement for—the Executive's ordinary creation authority. **Project-owner decision, 2026-09-16:** the former special petition supermajority threshold is removed; there is no longer a standalone special-percentage or special-denominator rule for this route.
* Every election/motion has an immutable opening roll of eligible identities for that electorate. Newly created identities or newly admitted body members cannot enter an already-open roll.
* At finalization, **B** is the set of opening-roll identities whose valid accepted ballot exists, including a valid abstention. **U** is the set of opening-roll identities with no valid accepted ballot that remain eligible. The sets are disjoint and each identity appears at most once. `D = |B| + |U|`.
* Threshold arithmetic is integer-exact: 60% = `ceil(3*D/5)`, two-thirds = `ceil(2*D/3)`, three-quarters = `ceil(3*D/4)`, strict majority = `floor(D/2)+1`, unanimity = D; no threshold vote passes when D=0.
* The first valid accepted ballot is immutable and cannot be withdrawn/replaced by the voter or a clerk. Rejected/malformed submissions may be corrected before close only while the opening-roll identity remains eligible; rejected attempts remain preserved. Abstention is a valid ballot in B/D but never affirmative.
* Franchise belongs to each living participating development identity unless excluded by the controlling rules. Game players, nonexistent/naturally dead/capital-terminated identities, identities under a **formal proceeding**, permanently felony-disenfranchised identities, E-COAIAs, L-COAIAs, and CRA identities may not cast new ballots. A mere allegation, ordinary defect report, retirement, scheduling pause, dissent, C4 referral, or bare allegation is not by itself disqualification; for C4, temporary ineligibility begins only with a court `C4_CREATION_EVENT_OPEN` and authenticated affected-identity list.
* Death, charges, or another disqualification **after** a valid ballot leave that ballot in B and the voter in D for that election. Pre-ballot disqualification removes the identity from U while ineligible. If a temporary restriction ends before closing, an otherwise eligible opening-roll identity without a ballot returns to U; restoration after closing does not reopen the result.
* A lawful felony conviction creates the permanent franchise bar immediately when the authorized court or Senate enters the conviction; appeal/sentence finality is not required for that voting consequence. Capital termination remains stayed until the meaningful appeal opportunity and sentence finality required by the controlling case rules.
* Population/vote totals used for constitutional or institutional action must be derived from authoritative eligibility/census state, not UI counts, forum reactions, or hidden duplicate identities.
