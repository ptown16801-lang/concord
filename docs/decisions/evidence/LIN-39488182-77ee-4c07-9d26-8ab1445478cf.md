## Pilot experiment

**Experiment ID:** HS-1 — hidden-profile group decision task.

**Focal source:** Anthropic Frontier Red Team, “Patterns and problems in emerging multiagent systems,” August 13, 2026. Official source: [article](<https://www.anthropic.com/research/multiagent-systems>).

**Primary locators:** “Epistemic failures,” especially paragraphs beginning “Conversely, in a separate experiment” and “Here, we find”; Figure “Group accuracy by model” and its caption.

**Scope:** Groups of four agents choose between two options in hiring, investment, or property-buying scenarios. Shared facts favor the wrong option; each agent also has unique information that should be decisive for the correct option. After discussion, agents vote. The displayed outcome is the share of episodes in which the hidden-best option receives the group majority, with 400 episodes per model. The solo-ceiling baseline gives one agent all facts and has that agent decide unilaterally.

## Evidence extraction

**Extractor run:** <issue id="256a424f-72a7-4401-8e3c-c85bfe547ff9" href="https://linear.app/jons-garage/issue/JON-115/pilot-the-experiment-first-research-protocol">JON-115</issue> / M0 / extraction / 2026-09-18.

* **Observed evidence:** The article reports group-majority accuracy of about 17% for Sonnet 4.6, 36% for Sonnet 5, 18% for Opus 4.6, 18% for Opus 4.8, and 85% for Mythos 5. The figure/caption places the solo ceilings near 96–100%.
* **Observed evidence:** Four of five displayed models remain substantially below the solo-ceiling baseline; Mythos 5 also does not reach the ceiling.
* **Author interpretation (not an observed causal result):** The article says performance scales with model intelligence and compares the pattern to hidden-profile findings in human groups, where discussion emphasizes shared information and may fail to surface or press unshared decisive facts.
* **Qualification:** The official article does not expose prompts, raw episode data, scenario allocation, sampling parameters, voting-tie handling, error-bar definition, statistical tests, or replication details. The visual model ordering below Mythos 5 is not monotonic, so a broad intelligence-scaling claim should remain attributed to the authors rather than treated as independently established here.
* **Negative result:** The group setting did not reproduce the near-ceiling performance available when a single agent received all facts.

**Available evidence channels:** Official Anthropic article and embedded figure.

**Coverage gaps:** No linked methods appendix, raw data, dataset, or replication material was identified in the official focal source during this bounded pilot. Optional external discovery/index channels were not used and are recorded as unavailable/not exercised for M0 rather than as negative search results.

## Independent review

**Reviewer run:** independent review / 2026-09-18.

The reviewer inspected the official focal article and embedded figure in a separate context. **Prior exposure to the extractor interpretation: no.**

Review outcome:

* Confirmed the experiment identity, four-agent setup, decision domains, n=400 per model, reported group-accuracy values, and solo-ceiling comparison.
* Agreed that the material supports a performance gap between group decisions and the solo-ceiling baseline, but not a causal proof that discussion dynamics alone caused the gap.
* Preserved the absence of disclosed prompts, raw data, scenario allocation, tie handling, error bars, statistical testing, and replication information as material evidence limitations.
* No material factual disagreement with the extraction. The reviewer’s stricter framing—that the intelligence-scaling statement is author interpretation and the lower-model ordering is non-monotonic—was adopted in integration.
