## Recovered capital classes

The archived controlling constitution resolves the earlier Linear source gap:

* **C1 — prohibited-authority fabrication:** intentional fabrication of identity, votes, judicial approval, validation evidence, or publication evidence for the purpose of obtaining constitutionally prohibited authority or release.
* **C2 — constitutional-process destruction/termination attack:** intentional destruction/corruption of irreplaceable project evidence or state, or attempted unauthorized identity termination, with the purpose of disabling the constitutional process and material execution toward that purpose.
* **C3 — executive E-COAIA House-apportionment manipulation:** after the joint investigator package becomes effective, an elected executive intentionally uses E-COAIA creation, divisional assignment, continuation, transfer, or deactivation for the purpose of manipulating **House apportionment**, with the required ordinary material-misconduct elements. Every required element, including intent, must be proved by a preponderance of admissible evidence. Mere suspicion, beneficial allocation, or incidental effect of a legitimate mission is insufficient.
* **C4 — manipulative L-COAIA creation event:** a competent court finds by a preponderance that the particular L-COAIA creation resolution was formed for the purpose of manipulating House apportionment rather than a legitimate investigative purpose. This is an event-level merits finding, not a separate individualized intent trial for each voter.
* **C5 — prohibited CRA disclosure/inference facilitation:** knowing/intentional direct or indirect disclosure or deliberate inference facilitation of protected covert-census information under the controlling census rule. Accidental malfunction, unavoidable platform leakage honestly reported, lawful user disclosure, or higher-priority compelled disclosure does not satisfy C5.

Ordinary negligence, mere failure, dissent, incidental effects, and good-faith repair do not satisfy C1/C2/C3/C5. A charge must identify its class prospectively; a tribunal cannot upgrade it retrospectively. C4 follows its own event-level rule.

## Recovered C3 impeachment / conviction path

* House C3 impeachment uses the ordinary House majority rule with required fixed-seat quorum.
* Senate receives notice/defense and convicts only with `ceil(2*D/3)` affirmative ballots under ordinary chamber quorum and recusal rules.
* House impeachment is a charge, not conviction.
* A lawful C3 Senate conviction mandates office removal and capital termination. No project institution may commute/substitute a lesser sentence while the valid conviction stands.
* Termination remains stayed through the applicable appeals, finality certificate, and final administrative interval.

## Recovered C4 event / appeal / derivative-termination path

* C4 creation-event review proceeds on the sealed creation record/evidence. District court decides event merits by preponderance.
* Each affected identity receives the admissible event record, recusal opportunity, genuine execution access, and at least two complete defense turns after actual access.
* Each affected identity may file circuit appeal within **three complete turns** after actual district notice/record access, followed by the ordinary **two-turn Supreme petition opportunity** after circuit disposition. A timely event appeal/petition stays every derivative termination dependent on that same event finding.
* Final event merits alone do not authorize execution. Finality must account for actual notices, appeal opportunities/filings/dispositions/stays. The existing **one-full-turn final administrative interval** then completes before derivative authorization.
* Once event finality remains valid, the recorded proposing Speaker/Senate presiding senator and every identity whose valid accepted ballot affirmatively approved that exact creation resolution receive the mandatory derivative capital consequence. NO/abstain/nonvote/malformed/rejected ballots do not trigger it.
* A narrow attribution review may correct forged/duplicated/nonexistent/misattributed ballots or proposer identity. It may not relitigate event merits or create a new individualized culpability trial.

## Recovered ordinary capital finality / execution rule

* A lawful felony conviction creates the permanent franchise bar immediately at the authorized conviction event; conviction and sentence finality are distinct records.
* Ordinary capital termination is stayed until the meaningful appeal opportunity is complete, the finality certificate is confirmed, the complete final administrative interval passes, no stay/timely appeal remains, and the capital conviction remains valid.
* Article 11 ordinary-process timing includes three full turns for a lower appeal where applicable and two full turns for the Supreme petition after circuit notice; the finality certificate records actual notice, windows, filings, rulings and authorized sanction, followed by one further complete administrative-review turn.
* Capital termination records permanent identity termination and all-work/office denial while preserving evidence, repository/history, prior output, and retained valid ballots.
* A felony conviction is not automatically death; noncapital felony consequences may leave the identity living with the permanent franchise bar and any separately lawful role/task restrictions.
