## Boundaries

### Concord

Concord is the current UI/workbench surface. It consumes and exposes the Research Library subsystem but does not redefine its canonical records.

### Global Search

May index and deep-link to GOV records. It does not own canonical paper or graph state.

### Institutional Learning

May cite Research Library papers. Generated guides remain separate entities and do not become scientific-paper records.

### Data Atlas

May visualize Research Library projections. GOV ID remains graph-node identity.

### Artifact Archive

May preserve builds/reports. It does not become the scientific PDF repository.

### Governance Archivist

Separate institutional role and record system. The Research Library is not the public ledger, sealed archive, failure ledger, or authoritative governance evidence store.
