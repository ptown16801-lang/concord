## September 2026 — governance foundation

Concord became the working interface and research environment for a persistent multi-agent governance model. The architecture was separated from any obsolete software-development framing. Current artifacts use the Multi-Agent Governance Model identity only.

Core architectural decisions established during the governance interview included persistent agent identities, a privileged non-agent mechanical scheduler, durable per-agent checkpoints, authoritative registry/current-state precedence, complete attributable raw interaction history, isolated private memory/work roots, Total Recall as a residency/resource state rather than death, and final agent death with optional pre-death transfer of transferable state to a distinct successor identity.

The scheduler design evolved into a split model: an opaque global capacity allocator plus protected domain-local schedulers. Public scheduling is visible through authorized read-only views while protected identities and missions remain within their security domains. Urgency is democratically determined; the scheduler mechanically allocates based on lawful priority, executable work, bottlenecks and resources.

Security design converged on multiple institutional gateways, domain-local authorization services, dedicated writer services per protected domain, append-only publication through an independent Archivist, domain-separated protected backups, and explicit freeze/evidence-preservation before recovery of a corrupted authoritative domain. Microsoft Agent Governance Toolkit was selected as the primary governance/security framework, with stronger OS/process isolation and sealed privilege domains retained.

The independent legality checker was separated from scheduler optimization. Mechanical violations block automatically; interpretive ambiguity proceeds to lawful adjudication. A same-domain standby is permitted only as a verified availability replacement, not a second opinion. If primary and standby are both unavailable, ordinary affected work freezes and only categories designated in law beforehand may continue as essential continuity.

Agreement governance was developed as a first-class subsystem. Binding agreements require exact mutual assent plus authoritative registration. Agreement class determines scope. Higher law or higher lawful authority controls conflicts; same-level instruments require explicit supersession or reconciliation/adjudication. Breach uses a cure-first model. Failed cure makes the dispute eligible for adjudication while unaffected obligations continue. Amendments are append-only. Committee agreements survive membership changes; dissolution terminates them unless lawfully assumed. Inter-institution obligations can survive disappearance of one party on the surviving party’s side. Transfer of an agreement while the institution still exists remains an open decision.

Relational analysis was explicitly constrained: do not infer political position or motive from isolated text. Compare actors on matched underlying events/propositions, beginning with the immediate local group and moving outward through institution, peer groups and overall population. Separate formal affiliation, observed behavioral alignment and temporal trajectory.

## September 14–15 — Concord interface expansion

Concord expanded from architecture into a combined interactive interface. Work focused on integrating the forum, educational/instructional surfaces, research archive and highly visual data displays into a single workbench rather than separate sites.

A forum was designed with a deliberately small-community visual style, topic boards, groups, threads, recent posts, account and verification discussions, and non-binding social reactions. Forum-post votes and article helpfulness signals are explicitly separate from constitutional ballot records; they may be used for unofficial consensus analysis only.

A relational/behavioral observatory was developed around dyads, matched-event comparisons, interaction telemetry, helpfulness/reaction data, temporal alignment and control-panel views. The design emphasizes descriptive relationship analysis rather than automatic motive or loyalty claims.

The decision-propagation project introduced dynamic visual pathways for major institutional actions. The intent is to represent roughly dozens of major decision types as data-driven subgraphs with guarded state transitions, replay, comparisons and interactive propagation through institutions.

The Workbench evolved into a mobile-first, no-scroll atlas with many clickable data portals, rotating data sculptures, synchronized shared timelines, dense chart panels, graph inspectors and a MIDI/audio layer. Rotating geometry is data-sized: mean data controls scale while deviations stretch/compress the surface. Later interaction work added free multi-axis rotation, pinch/zoom, tilt-linked intensity, time movement, interactive temporal-field behavior and faster synchronized traces.

Audio work embedded the supplied MIDI archive locally, used crossfades when entering different data spaces, and added short randomized click phrases derived from the local MIDI material.

A large institutional learning library was generated for Concord: a dystopian-styled fictional welcome; a 930-page hashing guide; a 35-page archive guide; and 12 major government-function guides at 100 pages each, totaling 2,165 numbered pages. These materials were intended to become searchable, indexed in the Workbench and opened in floating library windows.

The next interface design round moved toward a left-side file-explorer/navigation column, global indexed search with autocomplete, resizable/stackable/snap-capable floating windows, contextual question-mark help on moving/interactive assets, graph-specific crosshairs and pan/zoom controls, and a richer animated background/data fabric. Graphs are to be evaluated individually for meaningful representative data, unique visual methods, scaling and sensitivity rather than repeated filler visualizations.

## Economy and marketplace research

The project began a separate economy/marketplace track focused on agent barter, skilled labor, deal formation, political/relational context and intention analysis. External implementation research is constrained to free or open-source options where components are selected.

A foundational architecture decision rejects blockchain as the marketplace substrate. Concord retains useful cryptographic primitives—signatures, hashes, hash-linked records, Merkle commitments, atomic settlement and append-only provenance—inside the existing authoritative-store and Archivist architecture. Blockchain can be reconsidered only if genuine decentralized consensus among mutually distrustful authorities becomes a requirement.

## September 15 — challenge, dispute, and quiz-correction design

A new Concord challenge/correction workflow was defined for logged-in users who believe quiz questions, explanations, citations, project-consistency claims, or other instructional content are wrong or unsupported. The implementation owner is JON-45 (private source reference withheld), **Challenge, dispute, and quiz correction workflow**.

The working design uses version-linked challenge cases so the original content, attempt, evidence snapshot, and later correction remain attributable. Challenge classes distinguish factual error, project inconsistency, superseded information, citation problems, ambiguous questions, wrong answer keys, explanation defects, insufficient evidence, and proposed project changes. Proposed changes are routed to discussion rather than being mislabeled as factual corrections.

The proposed state flow is `Submitted → Duplicate Check → Triage → Evidence Review → Independent Review → Resolution → Repair → Published`, with explicit unresolved/appeal states. Waiting time may increase queue priority but never creates a substantive verdict. Queue priority is deliberately separate from truth.

Scoring is separated into learning/mastery, challenge contribution, reviewer reliability, and optional confidence calibration. Filing a challenge does not automatically improve a quiz score, and an ordinary good-faith rejected challenge is not itself a reason for a penalty. Quiz-repair behavior preserves the original attempt and supports regrading, zero-weighting, explanation repair, or other deterministic corrections after review.

The plugin workflow was also defined: Linear acts as the project/control plane; the existing Google Drive research archive supplies stable evidence references without creating a duplicate scientific library; GitHub becomes the implementation/CI history once the canonical Concord repository is identified; and Figma may be used for UI iteration. The connected GitHub account does not currently expose an unambiguous Concord repository, so repository selection remains an explicit implementation dependency rather than being guessed.

Pilot coefficients for queue priority, reviewer weighting, review SLAs, reviewer quorum, appeal rules, privacy of attempt-linked disputes, and automatic reuse of resolved cases as advanced quiz material remain provisional until tested.

## September 15 — clarification of information-dispute subsystem

The information-dispute feature was clarified after an earlier design incorrectly gave Concord correction/adjudication authority. The controlling intent is observational: a logged-in participant may report or dispute a quiz question, explanation, citation, project-consistency claim, or other addressable Concord information, and Concord records the report and associated evidence/signals for later analysis.

Concord does not decide through this subsystem that the disputed content is true or false; does not automatically alter underlying content, answer keys, explanations, or quiz scores; does not regrade attempts; and does not treat community consensus as truth. The core model is `Report → Responses → Signals → Analysis`.

Reports preserve the exact target identity and version/snapshot. Independent duplicate reports are retained as distinct observational events and distinguished from support expressed after exposure to an existing report. Potential research stages include independent/blind evaluation followed by visible discussion so temporal changes in position can be measured. JON-45 (private source reference withheld) was renamed **Information dispute and reporting system**. Earlier correction/regrade/adjudication concepts are retained only as superseded historical context and must not govern implementation.
