# Owner decision: no Research Library section in the Concord app

This sanitized summary records a decision the project owner made on 2026-11-25 in a private chat with an agent (original kept privately). It is a derivative, not an unchanged original.

The owner decided that the Concord app will have no Research Library section. The owner said the research papers are organised in folders, named no fixed place for them, and said no app section will be built for them. The research library itself, as papers in folders, is still Concord work.

The owner approved the exact text of CON-099 and the matching changes to CON-001, CON-065 and CON-073 before they were written. This record decides nothing else.
