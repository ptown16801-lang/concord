# Population and voting

The design retains a forty-founder baseline and a ceiling of 300 living governance identities. The founding count is inherited from the preserved project decision/register; the exact original numeric-approval provenance remains qualified. Population is distinct from simultaneous execution. Ballot accounting distinguishes B, U and D and cannot pass when D is zero. C3 Senate conviction requires clear and convincing evidence for every required element, including intent; C4 keeps its separate preponderance standard.
